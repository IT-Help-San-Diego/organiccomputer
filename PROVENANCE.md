# Provenance of the images

How each image in this repository was made. The License section of the README rests on this record.

## `assets/brain.webp` and `assets/brain.jpg`

Generated on 26 June 2026 at 18:37 UTC by the image model FLUX.2 [klein] 9B from Black Forest Labs, hosted by fal.ai, called through the image-generation tool of the Hermes agent. The prompt was written by Claude Opus 4.8, the agent's language model, at the site owner's request for a modern brain for the datasheet. No image was given to the model as input.

The model returned one PNG of 1024 by 1024 pixels. It was resized to 880 pixels and saved as WebP (quality 82) and as JPEG (quality 84). The PNG is not kept in the repository.

The prompt, in full:

> A breathtaking modern anatomical human brain, three-quarter view, rendered as a hybrid of organic tissue and glowing printed-circuit-board traces. The left hemisphere is realistic soft organic brain matter in warm ivory and subtle gold; the right hemisphere dissolves into intricate luminous PCB circuitry, gold and copper conductive traces, tiny SMD components, and softly glowing nodes following the gyri and sulci folds. Thin gold filaments and neuron-like dendrites bridge the two halves. Set against a deep near-black charcoal background (#0d1117) with a faint radial glow. Subtle warm gold (#d4a853) and copper (#c8956a) accent lighting, with a few cool blue (#58a6ff) circuit highlights. Premium, scientific, Apple-keynote product aesthetic, photoreal yet elegant, ultra-detailed, centered, dramatic studio lighting, shallow depth of field, dark luxury tech datasheet hero image.

## `assets/og-card.jpg`

Generated the same day at 21:52 UTC by the same model through the same tool, from the prompt below. The result, 1024 by 576 pixels, was scaled, cropped at the centre to 1200 by 630 and saved as JPEG (quality 86).

> A premium 1200x630 social share card, wide cinematic landscape. A photorealistic human brain rendered half as warm organic ivory-gold tissue and half as glowing gold-and-copper printed-circuit-board traces, positioned on the RIGHT third of the frame. The LEFT two-thirds is clean empty negative space: a deep near-black charcoal gradient background (#0d1117) with a faint golden Fibonacci spiral and subtle thin circuit traces fading into darkness. Warm gold (#d4a853) and copper (#c8956a) rim lighting, a few cool-blue (#58a6ff) circuit accents. Moody, premium, scientific, dramatic, ultra-detailed, Apple-keynote dark luxury aesthetic. Leave the left side clean and uncluttered for text overlay. No text, no words, no letters anywhere in the image.
